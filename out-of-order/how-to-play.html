How to Play

One daily run. 10 rounds. About 3 minutes.

  1. 01
    ? ?

    Read

    Recognise the Movies & TV references on the cards. You usually won't need the exact year — just “I know this came before that.”

  2. 02
    A → B → C

    Decide

    Every round is a quick call — drag cards into order, tap the title that came first, or pick the one that stands out. On Timeline rounds, use the Move earlier / Move later buttons on every card.

  3. 03
    +100 base + up to 100 speed

    Score

    A correct answer is 100 points. Answer while the timer is high for a speed bonus up to +100.

  4. 04
    ⚠ Double Down ×2

    Risk

    Feeling confident? Activate Double Down ×2 before you submit. Right = double points. Wrong = zero, and your streak resets.

The timer never shows a hard fail

A big bar above every challenge counts down and turns red in the last 3 seconds. When it hits zero, the round closes with whatever you'd already chosen (nothing chosen counts as incorrect) and the results pop-up shows up — no “GAME OVER”, just the next challenge waiting.

The speed bonus scales with each round's own clock: answer in the fastest quarter of the time you're given for the full bonus, the next quarter for +75, and so on — a 30-second Order round and a 15-second Quote round reward “fast for this round” the same way.

Streak, Heat & Power-ups

Streak
Consecutive correct answers. A wrong answer sets it back to zero. Streak doesn't multiply your score — it drives Heat and unlocks power-ups.
Heat
Calm → Warm (streak 3) → Hot (streak 5) → On Fire (streak 7+). The environment gets louder; the challenge itself stays clean and readable.
+5 SEC
Unlocks at streak 3. Adds five seconds to the current challenge. One charge.
Reveal
Unlocks at streak 6. Reveals the release year of one card you choose. One charge.

Five kinds of challenge

Remember the moment, not the date — most rounds test recognition, not exact release years.

Timeline

Drag 3 cards into chronological order, oldest to newest. Partial credit if you get some of it right.

Which Came First?

Two titles, one question. Tap the one that came out first.

Pixel Scene

One pixel-art scene. Recognize it from three Movie or Series options.

Odd One Out

Four titles, three share something in common. Tap the one that doesn't belong.

Quote

A famous line. Pick which Movie or Series it's from, from three options.
